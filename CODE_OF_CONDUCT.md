# Code of Conduct

This team follows the [Climate Hack-tion Code of Conduct](https://hackjunction.app/participate/52/guide)
(Section 13 of the Participant Guide): treat teammates, mentors, judges, and organizers
with respect; no harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, or impersonation; keep
community channels on-topic; don't publish confidential or personal information.

Conduct or safety concerns during the event: `sharon.offenberger@gdsi.ie` or Discord
`@sharon_offenb`.
